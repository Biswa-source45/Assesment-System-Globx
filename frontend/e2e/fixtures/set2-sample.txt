Section A: Reasoning and English (4 marks)
Q1. Ravi ranks 12th from the top in a class of 40 students. What is his rank from the bottom? [1 mark]
(a) 27
(b) 28
(c) 29
(d) 30
Answer: (c) 29
Explanation: Rank from the bottom = 40 - 12 + 1 = 29.
Q2. Fill in the blank: The manager is responsible ______ the delay in delivery. [1 mark]
(a) of
(b) to
(c) with
(d) for
Answer: (d) for
Explanation: The correct collocation is 'responsible for'.
Read the passage and answer the next two questions.
Sleep is often treated as a luxury, but research increasingly shows it is a necessity. Experts therefore recommend a
steady bedtime routine over occasional catch-up sleep.
Q3. What is the main idea of the passage? [1 mark]
(a) Weekend lie-ins are the best cure for tiredness.
(b) Regular sleep is essential and catch-up cannot fully replace it.
(c) Adults should sleep exactly eight hours.
(d) Memories form only during sleep.
Answer: (b) Regular sleep is essential and catch-up cannot fully replace it.
Explanation: The passage argues sleep is a necessity and that a steady routine is better than
occasional catch-up sleep.
Q4. In the passage, the word steady is closest in meaning to: [1 mark]
(a) Unstable
(b) Early
(c) Quick
(d) Regular
Answer: (d) Regular
Explanation: A steady bedtime routine is one that is regular and consistent.
